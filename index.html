Is this an official Grok / xAI product?

No. Loopwork is an independent setup service. We configure AI assistants (including ones built on Grok) on your own accounts — we're not Grok, xAI, or any platform we connect you to, and none of them endorse us.

Is my data safe?

Every account stays yours — social logins, accounting platform, the AI assistant itself. We don't hold your passwords or store your data ourselves. For the financial-visibility service specifically, every connection is set up read-only by default, so the assistant can see your numbers but can't change anything in your books.

Do I need to be technical to run this?

No. Once it's set up, using it is just chatting with it in plain language — the technical setup is the part we do with you.

What happens after the setup session?

It's yours to run. We're not in the loop day-to-day unless you want an ongoing support plan for maintenance, troubleshooting, or re-training it as your business changes.
