# Client onboarding checklist

The client ends up owning everything — their own AI assistant access, their
own scheduler account, their own social accounts. Loopwork's role is to set
it up with them and hand it off, not to hold any of their credentials
ongoing. Do this live with the client (call or in person); don't ask them to
send passwords or verification codes over chat/email.

Confirm current sign-up requirements and pricing for each service below
before a session — provider plans and features change.

## 1. Discovery (once, ~30–45 min)

- [ ] What the business sells, who its customers are, its brand voice/tone.
- [ ] Which platforms matter (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, etc.).
- [ ] Any existing photos/videos they want the assistant seeded with.
- [ ] Who at the business will own the accounts going forward.

## 2. Accounts (the client creates and owns every one of these)

- [ ] Grok/X access for their business (whatever plan currently gives Grok
      Bot access — check xAI's current offering, this changes over time).
- [ ] Business accounts on each platform they want covered, if they don't
      already have them.
- [ ] An account with a scheduler Grok Bot connects to (e.g. Postiz,
      ContentStudio, AdaptlyPost).
- [ ] The client connects their social accounts to that scheduler
      themselves (OAuth sign-in) — never hand their login details to anyone
      else, including Loopwork.

## 3. Setup (done together, with the client driving the actual sign-ins)

- [ ] Connect the AI assistant to the scheduler account.
- [ ] Fill in [`HANDOVER-TEMPLATE.md`](HANDOVER-TEMPLATE.md) with what you
      learned in discovery, and feed the whole thing to the assistant as
      its starting context.
- [ ] Run one test draft through the full loop: assistant drafts → client
      approves inside their own chat → scheduler queues it → client
      confirms it looks right before anything goes live.

## 4. Handoff

- [ ] Client has used the chat themselves at least once, unassisted, before
      the session ends.
- [ ] Leave them a one-page cheat sheet: how to ask for a draft, how to
      approve/reject, who to contact if something breaks.
- [ ] Agree whether this is a one-time setup only, or includes an ongoing
      retainer for support/re-seeding brand voice later.
