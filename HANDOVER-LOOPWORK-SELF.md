## Who this is for

- Business name: Loopwork
- What they sell / do: A one-time setup service that configures an AI
  assistant on a small business's own accounts — either running their
  social media (drafting and scheduling posts) or giving them
  plain-language visibility into their own accounting data (read-only) —
  then hands full control to the business itself. Not an ongoing agency.
- Who their customers are: South African small business owners with no
  consistent social media presence, or no financial visibility beyond a
  spreadsheet, who can't justify hiring staff or paying an agency retainer
  for it.
- One thing that makes them different: the client ends up owning
  everything — their own accounts, their own AI assistant, no ongoing
  dependency on Loopwork unless they want a support retainer. Pay once for
  the setup, not a monthly fee forever.

## Brand voice

- Tone in 3-5 words: clear, direct, no-hype, reassuring, practical.
- Words/phrases always used: "your own", "once-off", "run it yourself",
  "it's yours to keep".
- Words/phrases never used: "revolutionize", "game-changer", "synergy",
  "disruptive", "10x".
- A real example of their own writing to match: "Your own AI assistant,
  running the busywork. We set up an AI assistant on your own accounts —
  your social media, or your financial numbers — connected, in your
  voice, answering to you. From then on, you run it yourself, one
  conversation at a time. No agency retainer, no new dashboard."
